    FEATURED PROJECTS

    NorthStar Tour and Travel

    Literacy of Love has an exciting new business, an all-inclusive travel agency that tailors excursions in Uganda, NorthStar Tour and Travel. In an effort to build sustainability, it is not only managed by and employs our own well-trained graduates, it also generates income for Literacy of Love. NorthStar will manage all travel details. All you need to do is come….we'll take care of the rest! We will transform dreams into memories.

    Visit NorthStar Tours

    Community Tours

    Meet the children of Literacy of Love at our Home, The Heart. Plan, organize and implement activities with our members. This is your chance to share love, friendship, and faith while creating memories which will last a lifetime.

    Mission Trips

    A mission trip is a journey of the heart. Our journey provides an opportunity to live love through action. What you'll receive is more than you can imagine. A mind stretched can never retain its original shape.

    Educational Tours

    Develop a bigger picture of the world by experiencing The Pearl of Africa first-hand. Learn about exotic and diverse new cultures, take a safari, raft the River Nile, tour the city, and witness rural village life.

    Our Impact in Pictures

    Children's Home

    LoL members come to visit, build relationships, learn about God and virtues, and enjoy family. We counsel and empower children to be the change in their family history.

    Farm

    We grow maize, cassava, matoke, and pumpkin, and we rear pigs and goats. This fortifies our diet and earns money.

    Maize Mill

    Our mill earns money, provides employment, helps us grind food from our farm, and serves as a valuable community resource.

    Serving Special Needs

    We support children with special needs which include non-verbal, deaf, phsyical limitations and learning disabilities. We also support children who are HIV positive.

    Supporting Families

    We work with families to provide basic needs and education while teaching them their emotional role is essential.

    Providing Education

    We support families by assisting with basic needs and eduation. We also counsel them about their valuable, emotional role and raise children together.
