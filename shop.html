Shop with Purpose

100% of our profits support our destitute and vulnerable children. We start small businesses to create self-sustainability and provide daily needs. We're not only building community; we're raising children to be successful community members who are equipped with a heart and skills to help others. With your purchases, you can make a difference, too.

If you are interested in a one-of-a-kind gift, you can shop locally or email us! Our products are always changing and are sometimes only offered once. Some products, like our pottery, is one-of-a kind made by a local potter, Tara Dedrick. Other products are hand-made in Uganda, some by our very own children, like Kudrah who was forced to quit school in 4th grade. Now, she's a very talented and skilled artisan and business owner.

Meet an Artisans

Kudrah's Story

Kudrah was forced to quit school in 4th grade due to poverty and family circumstances, but her story didn't end there. But her story didn't end there. Through Literacy of Love's entrepreneurship program, Kudrah discovered her incredible talent for craftsmanship.

Now, she's a very talented and skilled artisan and business owner. Her handmade creations not only provide for her family but also contribute to our mission. When you purchase one of Kudrah's items, you're not just buying a beautiful craft - you're supporting a success story and helping us create more like it.

"Education comes in many forms. Sometimes it's in a classroom, sometimes it's through learning a trade that transforms your life." - Cari Matejka
